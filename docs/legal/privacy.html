Laundri Privacy Policy

1. Scope

This Policy applies to personal information processed through the App and related backend services we operate or configure for the App. It does not govern third-party websites or services that we do not control (for example, your device browser, app store policies, or external sites opened from the App).

2. Roles and accounts

The App supports customer accounts and partner / launderer accounts. Depending on your role, we may process different categories of information (for example, business details and verification materials for partners).

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

  • Account and profile: name, phone number, password (handled by our authentication provider), role (customer or partner), and optional profile fields such as address, date of birth, and profile image where you choose to provide them.
  • Synthetic sign-in identifier: to support phone-based access with our authentication system, the App may associate your account with an internal email-style identifier used only for authentication and account linking.
  • Orders and service requests: pickup and delivery preferences, scheduling details, service selections (for example wash & fold, dry cleaning, tailoring), special instructions, pricing-related fields shown in the App, and order status history.
  • Messages and attachments: text you send through in-App chat related to orders, and images you attach in chat or upload for profile or business profile purposes where supported.
  • Partner / business information: business name, contact details, service descriptions, images, and similar content you submit as a Launderer.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and session data: information typical of mobile apps and our backend providers, such as device identifiers needed for push notifications, app version, and security-related logs maintained by our service providers.
  • Push notification tokens: a device token may be stored so we can deliver notifications (for example, when you receive a new chat message), using Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) on supported devices.
  • On-device storage: the App uses local storage on your device (for example, to remember onboarding completion and to persist your signed-in session). Certain preferences you save in the App may also be stored only on your device (see Section 6).

3.3 Location

The App may display maps and approximate locations based on addresses you enter, business locations provided by Launderers, and geocoding (turning addresses into coordinates). The App does not rely on continuous GPS location tracking for these features; map experiences use network-based geocoding and map content served over the internet.

3.4 Phone verification (where enabled)

Where phone verification features are presented, verification may be performed through our authentication provider or related services. Depending on the build and configuration, SMS or other carrier messaging may or may not be used.

4. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • create and secure your account;
  • provide core App features (discovering services, placing and managing orders, partner dashboards, chat);
  • send operational notifications (for example, chat or order updates) when you enable or accept push notifications;
  • improve reliability and security, detect abuse, and troubleshoot issues;
  • comply with law and respond to lawful requests; and
  • communicate with you about the App where permitted.

We do not sell your personal information. Firebase Analytics is disabled in the iOS Firebase configuration checked into this project; if analytics are added in the future, we will update this Policy.

5. Legal bases (where applicable)

If you are in a jurisdiction that requires a “legal basis” for processing (for example, the UK or EEA), we rely on one or more of the following, as appropriate: performance of a contract with you, legitimate interests (such as securing the App and preventing fraud, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights), consent (where we ask for it, such as for certain notifications or optional data), and legal obligation.

6. Payment-related information

Important: Depending on the App version, payment or billing details you enter may be stored locally on your device (for example, card brand or last digits, expiration, billing address fields, and similar entries the UI collects). Full primary account numbers are not intended to be persisted in plain form by the App’s local storage logic, but you should not enter real card authentication values (such as a security code) on a consumer device unless you understand the risks.

We do not represent that the App implements full payment card industry (PCI) compliant processing on its own. Actual payment processing may be added, changed, or handled by third parties in future releases. When live payment processing is integrated, this Policy will be updated to name the processor and describe what card data is collected and where it is stored.

7. Sharing and subprocessors

We share information with service providers that help us run the App:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage, realtime features, and optional serverless functions) for account data, orders, chat, images uploaded to our project storage, and related operations.
  • Google Firebase (FCM) for push delivery where enabled.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim and OSM map tiles (via third-party hosts) when you use map or address lookup features. Those providers may receive requests that include queries derived from addresses you search. Use of those services is also subject to their applicable terms and policies.

We may also share information:

  • with professional advisers (lawyers, auditors) where necessary;
  • with law enforcement or regulators when required by law or to protect rights and safety; and
  • in connection with a business transfer (merger, acquisition, financing), subject to appropriate safeguards.

The App may open WhatsApp or other messaging apps when you tap partner-provided links; those interactions occur under the third party’s terms and privacy policy.

8. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we will implement appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for cross-border transfers.

9. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account subject to Section 10 and applicable law.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict certain personal information, and to object to certain processing. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is consent-based, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@devorium.com. We may need to verify your request.

You can typically disable push notifications in your device settings. You can revoke camera or photo library permission in device settings, though some features may not work without them.

11. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age where parental consent is required in your jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. If you believe we have collected information from a child inappropriately, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated Policy in the App or on our website and update the “Effective date” above. Material changes may require additional notice where required by law.

14. California / U.S. state privacy notices (summary)

If a U.S. state privacy law applies to you, you may have additional rights (for example, to opt out of certain “sales” or “sharing,” though we state above we do not sell personal information in the traditional sense). Contact info@devorium.com to exercise rights. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics in a manner that would trigger additional opt-out rights beyond what the App’s features require.

15. Disclaimer

This document is provided to align with the App’s technical architecture as understood from the project codebase. It is not legal advice. Have qualified counsel review and adapt this Policy for your entity, jurisdictions, and actual data practices before publication.
